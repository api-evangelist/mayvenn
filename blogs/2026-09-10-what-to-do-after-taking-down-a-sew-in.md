Take a sew-in down in this order: oil the braid base, unbraid dry, detangle before any water touches your hair, then clarify, deep condition, and trim. Six to eight weeks under an install leaves roughly 2,000 to 5,000 shed strands at the braid base, and wetting that before you detangle is what causes matting. More
